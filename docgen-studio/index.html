Free Chrome Extension

DocGen Studio

Turn a live walkthrough into a shippable how-to guide in minutes

DocGen Studio is a free, local-first Chrome extension that records your clicks, captures annotated screenshots, and exports step-by-step SaaS guides as PDF or Markdown. No account, no cloud, no subscription. Optional AI-assisted drafting via your own OpenRouter key.

How to use (8 min guide) See how it works

Free forever · No signup · Local-first · BYO OpenRouter key

How it works — 3 steps, ~8 minutes

Pin the extension, click through your product, export a guide. No staging screenshots, no design tools, no manual writing. Each step below links to the detailed guide with screenshots.

01

Record your walkthrough

Click Start Recording, then use your product naturally. Every click is captured — even across page navigation. The REC badge confirms capture is active.

See recording in guide →

02

Edit & annotate

Each step gets an auto-annotated screenshot with arrow and label. Rewrite titles, add context, reorder steps, delete noise. Optional AI drafts copy from click labels.

See editor in guide →

03

Export PDF or Markdown

One click exports a polished guide. Markdown for GitHub/Notion/docs-as-code. PDF for email, onboarding packets, print. Zero cloud dependency.

See export in guide →

Built for documentation work

Four high-leverage use cases where DocGen Studio replaces hours of manual work with minutes of recording.

Help-center articles

Capture real UI instead of restaging screenshots. Record the actual flow, get annotated steps automatically, export to Markdown for your docs site.

Internal SOPs

Record an onboarding path once, share forever. New hires follow click-by-click guides. Update in minutes when UI changes.

Support macros

Export annotated steps showing exactly where to click. Reduce support ticket time. Customers follow visual guides instead of text-only replies.

QA walkthroughs

Hand a teammate a click-by-click record of the flow you just tested. Reproducible steps, annotated evidence, no "works on my machine."

Why DocGen Studio over Scribe, Guidde, or Tango?

DocGen Studio is the only free, local-first, developer-focused alternative. No per-seat pricing, no cloud lock-in, no mandatory AI credits. You own your data and your workflow.

Free forever

No subscription, no trial, no credit card. Scribe starts at $29/user/mo; Guidde at $16/user/mo.

Local-first privacy

Screenshots and recordings never leave your browser. Scribe/Guidde upload everything to their cloud.

BYO OpenRouter key

Use any model (including free tiers). No vendor AI markup. Scribe/Guidde bundle AI at premium.

Dual export: PDF + MD

Markdown for docs-as-code workflows. PDF for stakeholders. Most tools only do one.

Focused Crop (60%)

Auto-zoom on clicked element in dense UIs. Critical for complex dashboards where full-tab screenshots are unreadable.

No account required

Install and record immediately. No signup, no email, no team workspace to configure.
