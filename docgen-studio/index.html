Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions. Can't find what you need? Read the full guide or contact us.

Is DocGen Studio free?

Yes, DocGen Studio is completely free. No subscription, no account required, no hidden fees. Optional OpenRouter API key enables AI-assisted drafting (you can use free models on OpenRouter).

What browsers does DocGen Studio support?

DocGen Studio is a Chrome extension. It works on Chrome, Edge, Brave, and other Chromium-based browsers. Firefox and Safari are not supported at this time.

Does DocGen Studio send my data anywhere?

No. All recording, screenshots, and editing happen locally in your browser. Only click labels (e.g., "user clicked a button labeled Save") are sent to OpenRouter if you add an API key. Screenshots never leave your device. See our privacy policy for details.

Can I export both PDF and Markdown?

Yes. Export Markdown for version control (GitHub, Notion, GitLab, Obsidian) or PDF for sharing with non-technical stakeholders. Both formats include annotated screenshots.

How does DocGen Studio differ from Scribe, Guidde, or Tango?

DocGen Studio is free, local-first, developer-focused, and lets you bring your own OpenRouter API key. No cloud dependency, no per-seat pricing, no mandatory AI credits. You own your data and your workflow. See our detailed comparison.

Does DocGen Studio require an account?

No. Install and record immediately. No signup, no email, no team workspace to configure. All data stays in your browser's local storage.

What is Focused Crop (60%)?

Focused Crop zooms the screenshot around the clicked control so the annotation stays readable in dense UIs. Turn it off to keep the entire visible tab. Essential for complex dashboards.

Does recording work across page navigation?

Yes. Recording continues across in-session navigation on the site you are documenting. Stop recording from the popup when the walkthrough is done.

Can I edit AI-generated step text?

Yes. Every step title and instruction is fully editable. You can also reorder or delete steps before you export. AI is a starting point — you control the final output.
