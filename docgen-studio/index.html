Chrome extension

DocGen Studio

Turn a live walkthrough into a shippable how-to guide

Record clicks on any site, capture annotated screenshots, then export a step-by-step SaaS guide as PDF or Markdown. Optional AI drafts titles and steps from click labels using your own OpenRouter key.

How to use See how it works

How it works

01

Record

Start recording, then click through the product the way a customer would. Each click is captured, including after in-page navigation.

02

Annotate

Every step gets a screenshot with a thick highlight, label, and arrow on the control you used. Crop around the click or keep the full tab.

03

Export

Edit titles, reorder or delete steps, then export a PDF or Markdown guide. Optional AI can draft the copy from click labels only.
